Good toknow

Is chlorella the same as spirulina?

No. Both are sold as green supplements, but chlorella is a green alga, while spirulina is a cyanobacterium, often called blue-green algae. They are different organisms.

Why is it so green?

Chlorella is full of chlorophyll, the green pigment that plants and algae use to capture light.

Can I take it with my medicine?

Please show the box to your doctor or pharmacist before you start. Your doctor knows your health best, so please check with them before you change any medicine. Chlorella is naturally high in vitamin K, which matters if you take a blood thinner such as warfarin.

What does it taste like?

The tablets are swallowed with water, so there is nothing to sip. Noble Life to add its own description.
