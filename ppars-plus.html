03 / Before you start

General notes, not advice for you

Good toknow first

Please show the box to your doctor or pharmacist before you start. Your doctor knows your health best, so please check with them before you change any medicine.

Feeling unwell after taking any product? Stop, see a doctor and bring the box. In an emergency, call 995.

  • Contains chlorella, which is high in vitamin K. If you take warfarin or another blood thinner, talk to your doctor first.
  • Resveratrol may raise bleeding risk with blood thinners and antiplatelet medicines, especially warfarin (seen in lab studies, not confirmed in people).
  • If you have a hormone-sensitive condition, check with your doctor first.
  • Pregnancy, breastfeeding and children: there is no safety data, so please ask a doctor.
